How to explain the study

GA coordinates GDP/CTOT, crossing-time targets, speeds, short rejoining vectors and regional level caps. FCFS assigns CTOTs in fixed origin queues. Reference is the constructed schedule with the shared gate safety programme. All methods preserve departure queue order.

Both use the same initial traffic, routes, sector limits and safety rules. A sector is overloaded when its aircraft count exceeds its capacity. One extra aircraft for ten minutes is ten aircraft-minutes of overload.

Two synthetic scenarios. The saved run count is shown above. Passing spacing and runway checks does not mean sector overload is eliminated.
